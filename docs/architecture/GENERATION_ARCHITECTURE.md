# Creative Generation & Song Construction Architecture

**Domain:** `D06 — Creative Generation & Song Construction`  
**Architecture Version:** 5.0.0  

---

## 1. Creator Keyword Brief & Provenance (`CG-01`)

Creators supply creative concepts, seed titles, character/story elements, and keywords categorized by directive:
- `MUST_USE`: Mandatory lyrical inclusion.
- `PREFER`: Desired themes and motifs.
- `INSPIRE_ONLY`: Abstract atmospheric guidance.
- `AVOID`: Blacklisted words, slang, or tropes.

### Keyword Provenance Classes
1. **`USER_SUPPLIED`:** Directly entered by creator.
2. **`SOURCE_BACKED`:** Derived from verified slang corpora or theme intelligence.
3. **`AI_CREATIVE`:** Contextual suggestions generated by Gemini; clearly labeled to avoid confusing creative ideas with empirical truth.

## 2. Song Blueprint & Generation Pipeline (`CG-03` — `CG-10`)

```
Creator Brief (CG-01) + Selected Hit DNA Snapshot (HD-06) + Safe Artist Style (AR-04)
                                    ↓
                     Song Blueprint Assembly (CG-03)
                                    ↓
                       Hook Strategy Engine (CG-04)
                                    ↓
                   Modern Lyric Engineering Engine (CG-05)
                                    ↓
                   Suno-Style Sound Prompt Engine (CG-07)
                                    ↓
                   Generation Truth & Leakage Guard (CG-10)
                                    ↓
                   Validated Song Production Package
```

### Suno-Style Sound Prompt Constraints (`CG-07`)
- Strict character limit: < 1000 characters.
- Production feel: BPM, drum pocket, instrumentation texture, dynamics, vocal timbre.
- **Zero Artist Leakage (`AR-05` / `CG-07.M10`):** Real artist names, aliases, and trademarks are strictly scrubbed before prompt output.
