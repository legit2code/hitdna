# Modern Language, Slang & Vernacular Intelligence

**Engines:** `MI-08` / `CG-02`  
**Architecture Version:** 5.0.0  

---

## 1. Research-Backed Slang & Idioms

Modern language moves rapidly. The platform treats vernacular language and contemporary slang as a continuously researched intelligence domain:
- **Authority Sources:** Authorized dictionaries, curated lexicon corpora, and reviewed lyric transcriptions.
- **Data Attributes:**
  - `term`: The canonical word or phrase.
  - `meaning`: Explicit context-specific definition.
  - `regionScene`: Regional origin and subcultural association (e.g., Atlanta Trap, UK Drill, TikTok Viral).
  - `firstObservedDate` & `lastObservedDate`.
  - `trajectory`: `EMERGING`, `PEAK`, `STABLE`, or `OBSOLETE`.
  - `explicitness`: Strict clean vs. explicit categorization.

## 2. Era Authenticity Guard (`CG-02.M07`)
When a creator targets an era (e.g., 90s Grunge, 80s Synthpop, 2016 SoundCloud Rap), the system filters out modern anachronisms (e.g., prevents "rizz", "cap", or "bussing" from appearing in an 80s rock lyric generation).
