# Musical Pattern Intelligence & Abstraction Architecture

**Engines:** `MI-09` / `MI-10` / `CG-06`  
**Architecture Version:** 5.0.0  

---

## 1. Measured Musical Patterns

Harmonic, rhythmic, and structural patterns are analyzed from empirical observations and abstracted into safe, transposable building blocks:
- **Harmonic Roman Numerals:** Key-relative representations (e.g., $i - VI - III - VII$, $I - V - vi - IV$) and harmonic rhythm (bars per chord).
- **Groove & Rhythm Grids:** Kick/snare placement patterns, swing percentage, syncopation descriptors, and vocal density curves.
- **Arrangement Progression Templates:** Section duration ratios, chorus energy lifts, drop placements, and transitional elements.

## 2. The No-Copy Protection Invariant (`MI-10.M07`)
- The system abstracts patterns into generalized mathematical and harmonic structures.
- It is strictly forbidden to copy, reproduce, or output uniquely identifying melodic passages, copyrighted lyric sequences, or recognizable stems from specific protected works.
- Output generation instructions provide abstract structural blueprints for original song creation.
